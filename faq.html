Where do you offer doula support?

In-person birth and postpartum support is available in Houston and surrounding areas. Virtual preparation and support are available to clients across the United States. We’ll confirm your location and the available options on your intro call.

Which birth settings do you support?

Evelyn offers birth doula support for hospital, birth-center, and home-birth settings. Doula support works alongside your maternity care team and does not replace medical care.

Can my partner be involved?

Yes. Supporting and involving your partner or chosen support person is part of Evelyn’s approach. We’ll talk about how you’d like your people to be involved.

How much does support cost?

Pricing and package details are discussed after your introductory conversation, once your needs and availability are clear. Agreed services are booked and paid for through a private link.

Is the first call free?

Yes. Your introductory call is free and lasts 20 minutes. You can choose phone or video.

Is a doula a medical provider?

Doula support is nonmedical. Your clinical care team handles examinations, procedures, medical advice, and treatment. A doula provides personal support and helps make room for your questions and preferences.
